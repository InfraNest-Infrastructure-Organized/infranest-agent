# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume they are acting in good faith. Criticise the code, not the person who wrote it.

## What is expected

- Be welcoming and patient, particularly with people new to the project or to Go
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully, and give it kindly
- Focus on what is best for the people who run this software on their servers
- Show empathy toward other people in the project

## What is not acceptable

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing anyone's private information — a physical or email address, or anything else — without their
  explicit permission
- Anything else a reasonable person would take as inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions and code review — and whenever
someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report anything that concerns you to **conduct@infranest.app**. Reports are handled confidentially, and we
will not act against a good-faith reporter.

We may remove comments, commits, code and issues that do not follow this document, and may ban anyone whose
behaviour we judge inappropriate, threatening or harmful. We will explain moderation decisions when asked.
